Data practices

Privacy policy

Updated October 29, 2026. Capvolve Analytics is operated by the owner of the Capvolve YouTube channel. Contact: dmitri.d907@gmail.com.

Google data accessed

With the channel owner’s authorization, the application reads channel identity and aggregate video analytics: video views, engaged views, watch time, average viewing duration and percentage, audience retention, likes, comments and shares as aggregate counts, and subscriber gains and losses. Some reports may be unavailable or delayed.

The application requests youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It does not request permission to upload, edit or delete YouTube videos. It does not retrieve individual viewer identities or comment text.

Purpose and use

Reports are used to review the owner’s video performance, compare episodes and plan content. Aggregate reports may be reviewed by the owner and their authorized software or AI assistant for this purpose. Google API data is not sold, used for advertising profiles, or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Use and transfer of Google API data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

The collector runs on the owner’s Windows computer. OAuth credentials are encrypted with Windows user-bound DPAPI and kept outside the shared project folder. When credentials are transferred between the owner’s computers, they travel over an encrypted SSH connection and are encrypted again at the destination.

Aggregate reports are saved as JSON and CSV in the owner’s project workspace, which may be synchronized or backed up using the owner’s configured storage services. Reports and credentials are not served by this public website. Google handles sign-in; the application does not collect the Google account password.

Retention, control and deletion

Reports remain in the owner’s workspace until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the application’s Google access at Google Account connections and delete the local credential and report files. Revoking access stops future authorized collection after existing access expires; it does not itself delete previously saved reports. For assistance, contact the support address above.

This website

These static pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising or a sign-in form. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process connection information for hosting and security under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

This page will be updated when the application’s actual data practices change.
